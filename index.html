🛠️ How to Use Auslogics

When you open Auslogics for the first time, you'll see a clean, welcoming workspace. Here's a quick overview of how to start your first project:

  1. Create a new project: Click "New Project" and give it a name. Choose a template if you like, or start from scratch.
  2. Add audio tracks: Use the "Add Track" button to bring in audio files from your computer or record directly from a microphone.
  3. Edit and arrange: Drag and drop clips on the timeline. Cut, copy, and paste just like you would in a document editor.
  4. Apply effects: Open the effects panel to add reverb, EQ, or other enhancements. You can preview changes in real time.
  5. Mix and export: Adjust volume levels, panning, and other mix settings. When you're happy, click "Export" and choose your desired format.

Don't worry if you're new to audio production – Auslogics includes helpful tooltips and a built-in guide. You'll be creating music in no time.
